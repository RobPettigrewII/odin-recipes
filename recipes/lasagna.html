Description

This is a simple and delicious lasagna recipe that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar pasta sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
  • 12 lasagna noodles

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. In a large skillet, cook and stir ground beef until brown. Drain excess fat. Stir in pasta sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. In a separate bowl, mix ricotta cheese, egg, and Parmesan cheese.
  4. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Layer with lasagna noodles, ricotta mixture, and meat sauce. Repeat layers,
