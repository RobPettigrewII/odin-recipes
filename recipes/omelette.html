Omelette

Home

Description

This is a simple and delicious omelette recipe that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/4 cup shredded cheese
  • 1/4 cup diced vegetables (such as bell peppers, onions, and mushrooms)
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil for cooking

Steps

  1. Whisk together eggs and milk in a bowl. Season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat butter or oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet.
  4. When the edges are set, gently lift and fold the omelette.
  5. Place the omelette on a plate and top with shredded cheese and diced vegetables.
